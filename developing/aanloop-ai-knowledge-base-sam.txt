6. FAQ — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

Q1: What is an AI agent and how does it work?
A: An AI agent is a smart software assistant that can understand natural language, make decisions, and perform tasks automatically. Unlike a simple chatbot, an AI agent can take actions — like booking appointments, processing documents, or sending emails — without human intervention.

Q2: How much does an AI agent cost for my business?
A: Our solutions start from €997 for the Starter package. The exact price depends on the complexity, integrations, and maintenance needs. Use our ROI calculator on the website for a personalized estimate, or book a free demo.

Q3: How long does implementation take?
A: On average, 8 days from start to live. This is significantly faster than the industry standard of 8-12 weeks. Our speed comes from proven n8n workflows and ElevenLabs templates.

Q4: Is my data safe with AANLOOP AI?
A: Yes. We use self-hosted n8n workflows, which means your data stays on servers in the Netherlands. We are fully AVG/GDPR compliant (KVK 88606902). Your data never leaves the EU unless you specifically request otherwise.

Q5: Does AI work with my existing software?
A: In most cases, yes. We integrate with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Shopify, WooCommerce, Exact Online, Twinfield, HubSpot, Salesforce, and hundreds of other tools via n8n, Zapier, and Make.com.

Q6: What is the difference between a chatbot and an AI agent?
A: A chatbot only answers questions. An AI agent takes action — it can book appointments, process invoices, update your CRM, send emails, and make decisions based on your business rules.

Q7: Can I train the AI agent myself?
A: Yes, after implementation we provide training and documentation. For Enterprise clients, we also offer ongoing training and optimization services.

Q8: What happens if the AI doesn't understand a question?
A: The AI agent is designed to recognize when it's unsure and will either ask clarifying questions or seamlessly hand over to a human team member. You always stay in control.

Q9: Is AANLOOP AI AVG/GDPR compliant?
A: Absolutely. We are registered with KVK 88606902, all data is processed within the EU, and we follow strict GDPR guidelines. We can provide a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) on request.

Q10: Do you offer maintenance and support?
A: Yes. All our packages include standard support. Growth and Enterprise clients receive priority support with faster response times. We also offer monthly optimization packages.

Q11: What is n8n and why do you use it?
A: n8n is a powerful workflow automation tool. We use the self-hosted version so your data never goes to third-party cloud servers. This gives you maximum privacy and control.

Q12: What is ElevenLabs and why do you use it?
A: ElevenLabs is the world's leading AI voice technology. We use it for our AI Phone Assistant and voiceover services because it produces the most natural-sounding Dutch speech available.

Q13: Can I see a demo before deciding?
A: Yes! We offer a free 15-minute demo for every visitor. You can book it directly on our website or ask me (Sam) to help you schedule one.

Q14: Do you work with businesses outside Rotterdam?
A: Yes, we serve the entire Netherlands. While we're based in Rotterdam, our AI solutions work remotely for businesses in Amsterdam, Den Haag, Utrecht, and all other Dutch cities.

Q15: What industries do you specialize in?
A: We have specialized solutions for 11 industries: Real Estate, Hospitality, Legal, Freelancers, E-commerce, Accounting, Retail, Construction, Healthcare, Education, and Logistics.
