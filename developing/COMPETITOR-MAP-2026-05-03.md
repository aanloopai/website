# NL AI Agency Competitor Map (2026-05-03)

Compiled from live SERP research on Google.nl for: "AI bureau Nederland", "AI agency Nederland MKB", "AI consultancy Nederland", "AI receptionist Nederland", "AI telefoniste Nederland", "AI implementatie MKB". All entries verified by WebFetch on competitor homepages — no fabricated entities. Used for aanloopai.nl positioning + ranking strategy.

## Tier 1 — Direct competitors (full-service AI agencies for Dutch MKB)

### 1. AIFAIS — `aifais.com`
- **Positioning:** "De AI-partner voor het Nederlandse MKB" / "Verificatie-eerst AI-oplossingen"
- **Products:** Voice AI Agent (<200ms), Knowledge Engine (RAG, 99.2% claim), Digital Employee, Finance/Sales AI, web apps, SEO/GEO, ads
- **Pricing:** Hidden behind /pricing
- **Strengths:** Heavy sector + regio pages, 5.0 Trustpilot, name-drops Rijksoverheid/KVK/Rechtspraak, 8-week delivery promise
- **Weaknesses:** No public case studies on homepage, opaque pricing, no visible kennisbank depth
- **True competitor?** YES — clones almost the same product mix as aanloopai.nl (Marco/Emma analogues + sector pages)

### 2. AI Agency NL — `aiagency.nl`
- **Positioning:** "Bereken wat AI jouw bedrijf bespaart" — savings/ROI calculator first
- **Pricing:** Quote-based; €50K/jaar avg savings claim
- **Strengths:** Owns the exact-match brand domain, free ROI calculator + AI readiness scan, 50+ implementaties, 12+ sectoren, GDPR-compliance prominent
- **Weaknesses:** Tool-led not product-led (no Marco/Emma equivalent), no transparent pricing, generic "we begeleiden" messaging
- **True competitor?** YES — same MKB segment, same Dutch-only positioning, owns a critical commercial keyword via domain

### 3. AI-Bureau — `aibureau.nl`
- **Positioning:** "Maak jouw organisatie slimmer, efficiënter en menselijker met AI"
- **Services:** AI-notuleren, in-company training, consultancy, web apps, kansenonderzoek
- **Pricing:** Free intake; rates not public
- **Strengths:** Owns the exact-match keyword domain `aibureau.nl`, active kennisbank (ChatGPT/Gemini articles, success-factor downloads)
- **Weaknesses:** Training/notuleren-heavy — light on actual deployable AI products, no voice/WhatsApp suite
- **True competitor?** PARTIAL — overlaps on consultancy + training, weaker on operationele AI agents

### 4. Innoworks — `innoworks.ai`
- **Positioning:** "Wij maken je bedrijf AI-driven" — partner not consultant
- **Pricing:** AI-audit €2,250 / 14 days; agents start few €K; custom €6K–€40K; retainers
- **Strengths:** Transparent pricing, "AI Operating System" framing, case studies, 2–4 week delivery promise
- **Weaknesses:** No dedicated voice-AI receptionist product, smaller content footprint
- **True competitor?** YES — same MKB target, same audit→build→retain funnel as aanloopai

### 5. Appec — `appec.nl/ai-consulting`
- **Positioning:** "Eerlijk advies zonder dikke rapporten" — anti-Big-4
- **Pricing:** Free intake; scoping €1,500–€5,000; roadmap €5K–€10K (cap stated)
- **Strengths:** Transparent pricing, advice + build under one roof, recommends Copilot/Make/Zapier when maatwerk niet nodig — high trust positioning, active blog
- **Weaknesses:** Advisory-led, no own product portfolio (Marco/Emma type)
- **True competitor?** PARTIAL — wins on advisory keywords, loses on product keywords

### 6. AI-Agents.nl — `ai-agents.nl`
- **Positioning:** "Wij richten jouw personeelsbestand in met maatwerk AI-agents"
- **Strengths:** Owns the agent keyword domain, free AI-scan + free prototype, min 10 FTE filter signals real customers
- **Weaknesses:** Pricing entirely on aanvraag, thin sector content vs aanloop's 11 sectoren
- **True competitor?** YES — pure-play overlap with our custom workflow + agent products

### 7. DataNorth AI — `datanorth.ai`
- **Positioning:** "Use the power of AI, today" — Groningen/Amsterdam, both MKB and enterprise
- **Strengths:** 20+ deep case studies (Scania, Nestlé, manufacturing, fintech), strong domain authority, blog + ranking content (e.g. "Top 10 AI consulting NL")
- **Weaknesses:** Enterprise-tilted reference logos may scare smaller MKB
- **True competitor?** YES on consultancy SERP / PARTIAL on MKB price point

## Tier 2 — AI-receptionist / voice-AI players (compete with Marco)

### 8. Voicelabs (Robin) — `voicelabs.nl`
- **Pricing:** Robin Zelf Starten €299/mnd (1.000 belminuten + WhatsApp + chat); Op Maat custom
- **Strengths:** Calls + WhatsApp + chat in one agent (overlaps Marco AND Emma!), 81% deflection claim, transparent pricing, customer logos (Fraai Tandartsen, Franky's), Dutch-native voices
- **Weaknesses:** Single product brand, no broader AI-agency play
- **Threat level:** HIGH — most direct Marco competitor, undercuts our €597 with €299

### 9. Voxborn — `voxborn.com`
- **Pricing:** €20/mnd + €0.15/minuut, no setup, cancellable
- **Strengths:** "5-minute setup", knowledge base + sentiment + 085 numbers + 30-language transcripts, real screenshots of builder UI
- **Weaknesses:** Self-service play, no implementation hand-holding
- **Threat level:** HIGH for low-end / DIY MKB segment

### 10. SlimmeTelefoniste — `slimmetelefoniste.nl`
- **Pricing:** Kickstart €129/mnd + €1.290 setup → Pioneer €599+/mnd
- **Strengths:** Strong MKB+ZZP positioning, calendar integration, sector pages (lawyers, salons, coaches, makelaars), revenue-uplift claims
- **Weaknesses:** Setup-fee-heavy, lower price tier may signal lower quality
- **Threat level:** MEDIUM-HIGH — directly mirrors our "vanaf €597" entry point at top tier

### 11. MIKE365 — `mike365.nl`
- **Pricing:** €14.99 → €149.99/mnd (+ €0.55–€0.65/extra call) — explicit ladder
- **Strengths:** Cheapest visible on market, 14-day free trial no card, 10+ voice options, public tarievenpagina = SEO advantage
- **Weaknesses:** Very transactional, not a partner — limited integrations, no "agency" wrap
- **Threat level:** MEDIUM — drains the bottom of the market

### 12. Bonnie.ai — `bonnie.ai`
- **Strengths:** Amsterdam-based, hospitality vertical (Ron Gastrobar, Republiek Bloemendaal), 20+ talen, English-first brand
- **Threat level:** MEDIUM — niche horeca; not a full agency competitor

### 13. Klusio — `klusio.nl/features/voice-ai`
- **Pricing:** Voice-AI included in package vanaf €199/mnd
- **Strengths:** Trades-vertical (dakdekker, schilder, loodgieter, makelaar) — exactly aanloop's MKB audience, voice + chat + WhatsApp shared knowledge base, urgency detection
- **Threat level:** HIGH on bouwsector + vakman keywords

### 14. Flireo — `flireo.nl`
- **Pricing:** €0,25/min pay-per-use, no fixed contracts
- **Strengths:** Radical pricing transparency ("we tonen de provider-marge")
- **Threat level:** MEDIUM

### 15. AIKOM — `aikom.ai/ai-receptionist`
- **Strengths:** Voice cloning, 7.000+ tool integrations, claims 30% lead lift
- **Pricing:** Hidden
- **Threat level:** MEDIUM

### 16. Ekkovoice — `ekkovoice.nl`
- **Pricing:** Essential €749 setup + €249/mnd; Pro €999 setup + meer minuten
- **Niche:** Cosmetic clinics only — not a broad threat

## Schema / SEO sophistication observed

Direct verification was limited to homepage HTML rendered via WebFetch, but signals:
- **MIKE365** has a clean public **tarievenpagina** (price-table = `Product`/`Offer` schema candidate, ranks well).
- **Voicelabs**, **SlimmeTelefoniste**, **Voxborn** have **FAQPage**-shaped sections (visible Q&A blocks).
- **AIFAIS** has FAQ + testimonial blocks (likely `FAQPage` + `Review` schema).
- **DataNorth** runs structured case-study templates (likely `Article` + `Organization`).
- **None** of the voice-AI competitors visibly publish llms.txt or AI-citation optimisation — this is a wide-open lane for aanloopai.

## How we differ — our 5 strongest moats

1. **Product breadth + content density combined** — 171 pages × 16 diensten × 11 sectoren × 54+ kennisbank articles. Only AIFAIS comes close on the sector axis; nobody else matches the kennisbank.
2. **Marco + Emma as named, branded products** — most rivals sell "AI-telefonist" (generic noun); only Voicelabs ("Robin") and Voxborn brand the agent. Branded entities are AI-citation gold (Perplexity/ChatGPT cite named products).
3. **Strict EU-data + AVG + Rotterdam KvK** — we say it explicitly, AIFAIS/SlimmeTelefoniste mention compliance but nobody ties it to a verifiable KvK + Rotterdam HQ entity.
4. **Full-stack AI-agency + own products** — Innoworks/Appec/AI-Agents.nl are advisory-leaning; Voicelabs/Voxborn/MIKE365 are product-only. We're the rare both-and.
5. **Dutch-native tone + Nederlands-first content (not translated)** — DataNorth and Bonnie default English-first; we're Dutch-first which drives nl-NL SERP.

## How they win — top 5 gaps we should close

1. **Public, transparent pricing pages.** MIKE365, SlimmeTelefoniste, Voxborn, Innoworks, Appec, Voicelabs, Klusio — all show numbers. We say "vanaf €597" but a full `tarieven` page with `Product`/`Offer` schema would be a quick win.
2. **Concrete named case studies with numbers.** DataNorth (20+), AIFAIS (Rijksoverheid/KVK/Rechtspraak), Voicelabs (Fraai Tandartsen, Franky's). We need 5–10 case studies with logo + €/% outcome + `CaseStudy`/`Article` schema.
3. **Free interactive tools.** AI Agency's ROI calculator + readiness-scan, AI-Agents.nl's free prototype, MIKE365's 14-day trial. We need at least one interactive lead-magnet (ROI calc / Marco demo bot).
4. **Public AI-audit fixed-price product.** Innoworks's "€2.250 / 14 dagen audit" is a strong hook. Productising aanloop's audit at a fixed visible price would compete head-on.
5. **Trustpilot + G2 + Google reviews surfaced on page.** AIFAIS leads with 5.0 Trustpilot. Visible aggregated review schema (`AggregateRating`) is critical for both SERP and AI-citation trust.
